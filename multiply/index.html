Times tables for kids

Master the times tables, one handwritten answer at a time.

A space-themed multiplication game for kids. Quick practice for spare moments: the commute, the car, waiting for dinner. Then challenge the family in Versus, up to 4 players on one phone.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone · iPad

iPhone

iPad

Your child writes each answer by hand, right on the screen. Writing it out helps it stick, so it builds real recall instead of guessing from multiple choice. Tables 2 to 10 are free forever, with unlimited practice. No ads, no subscription, nothing to tap away by mistake.

01

Two ways to play

  • Speed Run: as many as you can in 60 seconds. Earn stars and beat your best score
  • Time Trial: answer every fact once, against the clock. Can you beat your best time?
02

It finds the tricky ones

Multiply spots the facts your child keeps missing, like 7 × 8 or 6 × 9. Tricky Ones gives extra practice on just those facts. Get one right three times in a row, nice and fast, and it flies away for good.

03

Unlock everything, once

  • Versus: 2 to 4 players race on one phone or iPad lying flat. First right answer scores
  • 12 × 12 tables: the next big challenge, tables 2 to 12
  • Pick Your Planets: every planet is a times table. Practise just the ones you choose
  • Tricky Ones: focused practice on the hardest facts
  • One purchase, shared with Family Sharing
04

Made for kids, built for parents

  • Friendly space adventure with Zip the alien
  • Handwriting is read on the device
  • Game Center achievements and leaderboards
  • Progress syncs across your devices with iCloud
Great for quick daily practice. Ten minutes a day turns times tables into facts your child just knows.
