Common questions

Where does the data come from?

From the National Environment Agency (NEA), through the data.gov.sg real-time API. Readings update every hour.

Which readings does KongQi show?

The official 24-hour PSI and 1-hour PM2.5 for Singapore's five regions: North, South, East, West and Central.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose KongQi. Each widget can follow your location or stay on a region you pick.

Does KongQi track my location?

No. Your location is used only on your device to pick the nearest region, and your coordinates are never saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
