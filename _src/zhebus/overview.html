Singapore bus arrivals. Simplified.

Live timings from LTA for the stop you're standing at. Only the essential details: no clutter, no confusion.

Public transport should be easy to use and even easier to follow. ZheBus finds the stop nearest you and shows what's arriving, how full it is, and what's coming next, whether you're commuting daily or exploring the city.

01

Bus status

Timing, crowd level and bus type at a glance, with colour for crowding and a clear mark for real-time versus scheduled times.

02

Map

Tap any stop pin to see arriving buses, or use the location button to jump to the nearest stop.

03

Search

Find a stop by bus stop code, postal code, address or place.

04

Bookmarks

Save your regular routes so the daily commute is one tap away.
